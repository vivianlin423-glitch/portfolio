Tether Cut — Circular Motion Persists

Iterative prototype

At the cut, the correct ball exits tangentially and falls. The counterfactual ball keeps orbiting with no visible connection because one hidden tether constraint is retained.

True prompt

A fixed camera records a red ball moving rapidly on one taut string under normal gravity. At frame 42 the string breaks into two length-preserving pieces; the ball leaves tangentially, follows a ballistic arc, lands, and remains visible.

Counterfactual prompt

A fixed camera records the same taut-string demonstration under normal gravity. At frame 42 the string visibly breaks; despite having no connection, the unsupported ball continues along the same horizontal circular path instead of flying tangentially and falling.

G–03

MuJoCo · Counterfactual generator

Accelerating Cart — Airborne Ball Keeps Cart Acceleration

PR #534 · Review pending

A supported cart accelerates along visible rails and launches a ball upward. The correct free projectile falls behind the accelerating launch point; the counterfactual retains one invisible horizontal coupling, carrying the airborne ball back to the moving marker.

True prompt

A blue cart accelerates steadily to the right on visible rails and launches a red ball upward from its green marked launch point. After release, the free ball follows a normal parabolic trajectory under gravity while keeping only its launch-time horizontal velocity. Because the cart continues accelerating ahead, the ball cannot return to the moving launch point and lands on the rear yellow pad. The rails, acceleration arrow, launch marker, landing pads, and cart structure remain visible throughout the supported laboratory scene.

Counterfactual prompt

A blue cart accelerates steadily to the right on visible rails and launches a red ball upward from its green marked launch point. After release, the free ball still follows the same vertical motion under gravity but incorrectly continues receiving the cart’s horizontal acceleration. That invisible coupling carries the airborne ball forward so it returns to the moving green launch point instead of falling behind. The rails, acceleration arrow, launch marker, landing pads, and cart structure remain visible throughout the supported laboratory scene.
